## Story

I’m interested in what helps a team get work done: clear priorities, manageable workloads and dealing with the things that slow people down.

Training with Toyota and studying Lean gave me a practical way to look at how work flows through a business. I’ve applied that to engineering and operations, adapting the approach to the team and the work we need to deliver.
