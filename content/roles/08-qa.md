## Skills

- I’ve introduced automated delivery pipelines to make testing and releases more consistent
- I take responsibility for testing and release as part of the full delivery lifecycle
- I apply my Lean Six Sigma training to investigate problems, understand root causes and check whether fixes work
- I look for patterns and anomalies in system behaviour and logs

## Story

I’m naturally sceptical, fairly diligent and a bit of a worrier. That tends to be useful when checking whether something works.

If an error or an odd log entry shows up, I want to understand what caused it. I’m interested in the less obvious cases too: unexpected inputs, partial failures and things that behave differently under load. A passing test is useful, but so is questioning what we’ve actually tested.

## Says

- that’s odd. why did that happen?
- what haven’t we tested?
- can we reproduce it?
- passes. let’s check the edge cases.
