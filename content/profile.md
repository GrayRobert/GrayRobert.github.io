## Summary

I’m a CTO with an engineering background and a broad interest in how businesses work.

At Topflight Travel Group, I help shape business and technology strategy, lead the engineering team and stay involved in design, development and operations. I’m comfortable working through a decision with the board or a problem in the code.

I enjoy the overlap between design and engineering: understanding what people need, working out how the pieces fit together and building something useful. I care about improving how we work as much as what we deliver.

## Experience

### Topflight Travel Group | 2011 to present
#### Chief Technology Officer since December 2014

I joined Directski.com as a Systems and Software Engineer in 2011, became Technical Project Manager in 2012 and took on the CTO role in December 2014.

Directski.com merged with Topflight to form Topflight Travel Group, and my responsibilities grew with the business. I now lead technology strategy and engineering across the group, supporting a business with more than €50M in annual turnover.

My work includes modernising our reservation and web platforms, moving infrastructure to the cloud, developing the team and managing technology suppliers. I remain hands-on, from reviewing architecture and writing code to investigating production issues.

### IT Systems Administrator | Harmonia Publishing | 2010 to 2011

I managed ICT systems and day-to-day technical support for more than 60 staff at a national publisher.

### IT Administrator and Web Developer | Eumom | 2007 to 2010

I helped move the platform from Classic ASP to .NET and C#, and built websites and digital advertising campaigns.

### Technical Services Manager | ICE Broadband | 2006 to 2007

I built, trained and led support and installation teams for a wireless broadband provider serving remote locations.
