## Story

I enjoy the overlap between design and engineering: working out how something should feel to use, then understanding what it takes to build it.

I run discovery sessions and work with UI/UX colleagues to turn ideas into practical interfaces. I care about the details, but also the whole journey, whether someone can find what they need, understand their options and take the next step with confidence.
